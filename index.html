Partnering with L&D

A Strategic Toolkit for Designing Business Unit Training

1. Foundational Content (The "What")

This is the core knowledge your team needs to acquire. We partner with you to develop this content in various formats to cater to different learning preferences and manage cognitive load effectively.

Video Series

Short, engaging micro-learning videos focused on specific concepts or tasks.

Podcasts

Conversational interviews with internal experts, leaders, or high-performers.

Interactive Guides

Digital newsletters, intranet articles, or documents with embedded resources.

Key Documents

A centralised repository of key research, frameworks, and presentation slides.

2. Learning Environments (The "Where")

We structure the learning journey using a holistic framework to ensure a comprehensive experience. This approach provides a blueprint for how, when, and where learning can take place most effectively.

3. AI-Powered Learning (The "How")

This is where we can truly personalise and accelerate learning. We can integrate various AI assistants, including the bank's own Copilot tool, to provide instant support, feedback, and tailored learning paths.

AI Study Assistant

A bot trained on your foundational content that can answer questions, create summaries, and generate practice quizzes 24/7.

Personalised Feedback Bot

An AI that can review a draft email or report and provide instant feedback based on a defined set of best practices.

Synthesis & Research Bots

Tools to help learners quickly analyse complex documents or research topics, accelerating their time to competency.

Adaptive Learning Paths

Intelligent systems that can recommend specific content or activities based on a learner's progress and performance.

4. Live Experiences (The "With Whom")

Human connection remains a vital component of deep learning and behaviour change. We integrate collaborative and expert-led sessions to solidify knowledge, build networks, and provide contextual support.

F2F & Virtual Workshops

Hands-on, interactive sessions designed for skill practice, problem-solving, and team collaboration.

Coaching & Mentoring

Pairing learners with experienced practitioners or leaders for personalised, on-the-job guidance.

Webinars

Expert-led deep dives on specific topics, new updates, or complex case studies, with live Q&A.

Practical Example: A Blended Learning Journey

Let's imagine we're designing a 3-week training program for your team on a new Client Engagement Model. Here’s how we could structure it by combining elements from the toolkit.

Week 1: Laying the Foundation

Focus: Understanding the 'why' and 'what' of the new model.

  • Foundational Content: Learners consume a 5-part video series and listen to a podcast with the Head of Division.
  • AI-Powered Learning: Learners interact with an AI Study Assistant trained on the new model's documentation to answer their initial questions.

Week 2: Exploring with AI Tools

Focus: Learning how AI can make applying the new model easier.

  • Live Experience: A live webinar demonstrates how to use different AI bots (like a Research bot and a Synthesis bot) in their workflow.
  • Learning Environment (Secret Space): Learners complete a challenge: "Use Copilot to draft a client follow-up email." A Personal Feedback Bot provides instant suggestions.

Week 3: Application and Collaboration

Focus: Applying new skills in a real-world context and sharing insights.

  • Learning Environment (Performing Space): Learners apply the model in their daily work, supported by their managers and designated mentors.
  • Live Experience (Group Space): A final face-to-face workshop where teams share experiences, troubleshoot challenges, and present their successes.

Additional Foundational Content Ideas

These are additional content types we can create in collaboration with your business unit to form a robust knowledge base for your team.

  • Research Activity: Structured tasks that require learners to find and synthesise information.
  • Micro-credential Courses: Short, certified courses on specific skills or topics.
  • Short Course: A more in-depth, multi-module course on a broader subject area.
  • Presentation: A comprehensive slide deck that can be used for self-study or group presentations.
  • Coaching or Mentoring Series: A structured program connecting learners with experts for guided development.
  • Study Mode: A self-directed learning module with curated resources, guides, and self-assessment tools.
  • Immersive Environments: Simulations or virtual reality (VR) scenarios that allow for safe, hands-on practice of complex tasks.
  • Gamified Learning: Applying game-like elements (e.g., points, leaderboards) to learning modules to increase engagement and motivation.
  • Game Challenges: Specific, often competitive, tasks or scenarios designed to test knowledge and application of skills in a fun format.
